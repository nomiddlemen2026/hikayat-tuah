3D models used in Hikayat Tuah
==============================

hero.glb
  Rigged human body and animations from "Universal Animation Library" (free/standard edition)
  by Quaternius - https://quaternius.com
  Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain). Obtained via https://github.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library
  Reduced to the animations the game uses and compressed. The Malay costume (baju, sampin, tanjak),
  face and colours are added in code by js/chars3d.js.

nature.glb
  Palm trees, trees, bushes, plants, rocks, grass and flowers from "Ultimate Stylized Nature"
  by Quaternius - https://quaternius.com
  Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain). Obtained via https://github.com/walterpalladino/godot-quaternius-ultimate-stylized-nature
  Converted from FBX to glTF, simplified and compressed; bark and rock textures replaced with plain colours.

lib/gltf.min.js
  GLTFLoader, SkeletonUtils and the meshopt decoder from three.js r159 (MIT licence, see lib/THREE-LICENSE.txt),
  bundled as a classic script.

Thank you, Quaternius, for releasing these for free.
